# Whole-box selection — 3.0.24

Single-choice rows/cards now activate their existing selection button when their plain background, heading, icon or numeric cells are clicked. The shared enhancement covers planner and tutorial master rows, gear comparison rows (including expanded outclassed gear), guide location cards, individual equipment slots and character-wizard progress boxes. Native full-card buttons, checkbox labels, creature-picker options and ordinary selects already offer a full selection surface and retain their existing behaviour.

The enhancement counts selection actions within each supported box. Exactly one enabled selection gets the pointer/hover affordance. Boxes containing multiple selection options do not become one selector; in particular, an owned style card with many independently selectable equipment slots retains those separate choices. Unsupported/disabled gear rows remain disabled. Current choices are highlighted across the row/card, and keyboard focus on the original button outlines its box. No new nested buttons or table-role overrides are introduced: native selection buttons remain the accessible keyboard owners and still support Enter/Space.

Links, buttons, inputs, selects, labels, editable text and disclosure controls keep their independent actions. Disclosure content inside a card does not select the card while being read; an outer disclosure around an outclassed gear row does not prevent that row's selection. Cancelled/modified/right-click events and dragged text selections are not forwarded. The handler revalidates the live controls before dispatching their original button click and guards that forwarded event from selecting twice. Rerendered worker views and lazy gear popovers receive the same enhancement. Reduced-motion preferences disable the hover transition.

Original selection behaviour is reused: gear changes retain their creature-scoped calculations and validation, master selection still opens the associated blocks/skip section, and location selection still checks access and updates the prepared kit. Native applications, saved account facts and the calculation formulas are unchanged.
