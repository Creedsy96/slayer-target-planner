# Strict gear upgrades and defence sorting — 3.0.22

The slot comparison hides strictly outclassed permanent stat gear from its main list. A replacement must be available and usable in the displayed setup, have no lower single-target or aggregate DPS, and use the same ammunition pairing. Every attack, strength/damage, prayer and defence bonus must be at least as good; weight must not increase. Weapons also retain their attack category and handedness and cannot become slower. At least one bonus, weight or speed must improve. Equal items and items with any trade-off remain visible.

This is a conservative proof against reviewed permanent, non-consumable stat gear, not a claim that raw bonuses describe every item. `permanentStatGear` positively recognises ordinary metal equipment, plain bows, ordinary dragonhide/mystic/infinity equipment and listed stat-only accessories. Unknown effects remain unfiltered until reviewed. Degradable armour, charged gear, ammunition/thrown consumables, protected equipment, set-effect items, god protection, teleports and unreviewed utility cannot establish a strict upgrade. Equipment weights and combat bonuses come from the already pinned DPS-engine equipment data. Both comparison rows must be usable; an unwearable higher-level, missing or unsupported alternative cannot hide an item. No new runtime network requests or Wiki scraping were introduced.

The currently shown item always remains visible. A collapsed **Show N strictly outclassed items** section names each hidden item's upgrade and retains **Use item**, allowing intentional downgrades without filling the main list. Selecting a hidden item still goes through the existing protection/ownership checks and creature-scoped recalculation. A chosen weaker item becomes the visible baseline rather than disappearing. Saved gear selections and character exports are unchanged.

After the shown baseline, rows sort by aggregate DPS (single-target DPS where there is no stack), then prayer, then average relevant defence. The defence average uses each distinct listed NPC attack type equally: Stab/Slash/Crush individually, all three for unspecified Melee, plus Magic and/or Ranged when listed. Unknown attacks retain the existing explicit five-type fallback. It is an equipment-bonus comparison, not an incoming-damage estimate. A tiny numeric tolerance handles floating-point DPS ties; IDs only make otherwise identical rows deterministic. The optimiser's existing prayer-first/tank-first armour policy is unchanged.

Primary background references: [Wiki degradation mechanics](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Degradation), [item weight](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Weight), [rune equipment](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Rune_equipment), and the pinned equipment data under `vendor/osrs-dps/source/cdn/json/equipment.json`. The engine does not provide a complete item-utility/degradation catalogue; the positive recognition rule deliberately retains uncertain equipment.

## Verification

Production build, strict TypeScript and all **431 JavaScript tests** passed. Seven new regressions exercise all bonus dimensions, weight, attack speed/category/handedness, unknown and charged/degrading/consumable/protective equipment, unavailable upgrades, actual-DPS trade-offs, assumed gear, selected-item preservation, equal-stat handling, the DPS/prayer/defence ranking, multiple attack-type means, real owned-gear filtering, explicit hidden-item selection, and collapsed accessible rendering. Existing selection, ammunition, armour fallback, scoped-cache and planner tests remain covered by the full suite.

Synthetic browser QA used **Unlock warning QA** with a Hellhound at the Catacombs of Kourend. All listed body swaps produced **4.047 DPS**. The visible baseline was Proselyte hauberk (8 Prayer), followed by Dharok's platebody (**122** relevant defence) and rune platebody (**82**) at equal DPS and zero Prayer. Three lesser platebodies were collapsed, each naming rune platebody as its strict upgrade. Expanding the section and selecting adamant platebody preserved it as the shown baseline and started the creature/affected-master refresh. Reset restored the automatic setup. These observations use synthetic equipment; native applications and actual player data were untouched. Package/source parity and ZIP integrity are checked during release assembly.
