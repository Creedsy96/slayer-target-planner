# Combat-only equipment candidates — 3.0.23

Automatic owned/assumed equipment searches and the slot comparison lists now share a combat-relevance filter. Cosmetic and skilling-only equipment with no beneficial combat bonuses or reviewed combat utility is omitted before searching or comparing alternatives. Negative-only novelty equipment is also omitted. A positive attack, strength/damage, prayer or defence bonus is sufficient, so defensive and prayer gear remain available even when they do not increase outgoing DPS. Weight reduction, travel or skilling benefits alone are not treated as combat bonuses.

The filter reuses the pinned engine's `noStatExceptions` through the thin local export adapter. It adds reviewed survival/Slayer utility outside the outgoing-DPS model, including life/escape effects, task bracelets, God Wars protection, desert survival and light sources. The pinned upstream source is unchanged. Zero-stat protection that only benefits a matching encounter (for example a facemask, earmuffs, nose peg or lit bug lantern) is omitted from unrelated fights and retained when its slot requirement applies. Encounter/location requirements can explicitly retain required equipment regardless of its bonus table. Usability, ammunition compatibility and existing protection checks still apply.

Combat relevance does not imply every passive is included in the DPS number: special attacks, recoil, automatic task effects, incoming damage and movement retain the app's existing modelling limits. See the primary [Slayer equipment reference](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Slayer_equipment) and [Lightbearer reference](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Lightbearer) for examples of equipment utility that is not described by raw stat bonuses.

The filter changes combat candidates, not recorded inventory. Imported bank/container snapshots, manual quantities, character exports and the Bank & gear inventory remain intact. Old saved choices without a combat benefit for the encounter are ignored during search with an explanation; valid combat choices remain fixed. This avoids invalidating the whole style or falling back to manual timing just because an older character pinned a cosmetic item. Reset clears those saved choices through the existing control. New comparisons do not reinsert cosmetics as selected or outclassed alternatives. The existing strict-upgrade filter, DPS/prayer/relevant-defence order and creature-scoped selection refresh remain in place.
